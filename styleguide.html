What we don't do

Rules

  • Reading measure capped at 68 characters. Serif gets slightly tighter leading than sans.
  • Tracked caps appear only as section labels in the rail — the brand's one typographic signature. They are not used for headings, buttons, or navigation.
  • Sentence case everywhere else, including buttons and links.

The rail layout

Section labels sit in the left margin the way notes sit in the margin of a board paper. Below 860px the rail collapses above the content and the margin note is hidden.

What we don't do
Body column, capped at 68ch. The rail is sticky on desktop so the reader always knows which section they are in.

Buttons and links

One primary action per view. Button text says what happens, in sentence case, with no arrow appended.

Arabic and RTL

The Arabic site is a mirror, not a translation layer bolted on. Use CSS logical properties throughout — margin-inline-start, not margin-left — so the same stylesheet serves both directions.

ليدرا شركة استشارية في الحوكمة. لا نملك اتفاقيات إعادة بيع، ولا نتقاضى عمولات إحالة من مزوّدي التقنية، ولا نحقق أي إيراد من التنفيذ.

  • Latin letterspacing does not transfer to Arabic — set letter-spacing:0 for Arabic labels.
  • Arabic text needs slightly more line-height and a slightly larger size to match Latin optical weight.
  • The logo does not mirror. The lockup order does: mark on the right in RTL.
  • Numerals: use Western digits for figures and dates unless the client asks otherwise, and keep them consistent across both sites.

What not to build

No carousels, no scroll-triggered fade-ups, no counters animating to a number, no parallax. No stock photography of handshakes, skylines, or people pointing at screens. No client logo wall and no testimonials until there are real ones to show. No chat widget. No pricing. No cookie banner, because there should be nothing to consent to.
