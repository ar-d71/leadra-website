كيف تُدار المهمة From first conversation to board readout.

أربع مراحل، وشريك حاضر في كل واحدة منها

  1. Diagnostic conversation

    Half a day with the people accountable for the decision. We tell you plainly whether we are the right firm for it.

  2. Proposal within five working days

    Scope, the outcomes we will be measured on, who delivers, the fee, and the terms.

  3. Delivery under partner ownership

    Work is carried out by partners, associate consultants, or vetted specialists. You are told who is on your engagement and in what capacity. A second partner reviews anything that reaches a board.

  4. Readout and measurement

    We agree before-and-after indicators at kickoff and report against them. No return-on-investment claims we cannot evidence.
