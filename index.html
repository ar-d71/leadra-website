What we do

Four practices.
One standard.

Leaders rarely arrive with a single, tidy question. Our practices are built to meet the whole of it — the person, the team, the appointment, and the board — and a partner carries the work from the first conversation to the last.

Leadership and ManagementExecutive CoachingExecutive Search and RecruitmentGovernance and Board Effectiveness

Is the team as strong as the strategy?

Leadership and Management

The effectiveness of a leadership team and the way its members work together. Management capability at senior levels. Readiness of the people who will lead next. And the tensions between executives or between functions that quietly hold a decision in place for months.

Most engagements here begin with a short diagnostic, so you can judge the work before committing to a programme.

Who does a chief executive think aloud with?

Executive Coaching

A confidential thinking partner for a leader carrying a decision alone. Coaching through a step up in scope, a first hundred days, or a change the organisation is resisting. Always built around an objective the leader sets, with a beginning and an end.

What is said in these conversations stays in them. The sponsor hears about progress, never about content.

Is the right person already inside?

Executive Search and Recruitment

Defining what the role genuinely requires, and what success in it would look like. Weighing the people you already have against it. Reaching, discreetly, beyond the organisation when the answer is not inside it. And staying with the appointment through the offer and the handover.

We start with the people you already have. A search that ends in an internal appointment is a good outcome.

Is the board seeing what it needs to?

Governance and Board Effectiveness

How well a board and its committees actually work, and what would make them sharper. Clear decision rights and mandates, so that ownership of a question is never in doubt. And the information a board needs in front of it to govern the risks that now sit on its agenda.

We work on how decisions are made and overseen, not on the systems beneath them.

On search

Over years of practice in the Kingdom we have built a circle of trusted executives — people whose work we know, not names on a list. Most searches begin there and inside your own organisation, and extend further only when the role genuinely calls for it. Our terms are agreed before the work begins, and our recommendation does not change with the outcome.
